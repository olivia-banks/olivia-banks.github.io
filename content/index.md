Hey, I’m Olivia. I'm a research developer building fast, reliable software for epidemiologists and
STLTs, mostly in C++, R, Python, and OCaml. I work under [George Vega Yon](https://ggvy.cl/) on
open-source modeling tools, including those behind ForeSITE, part of the CDC-funded InsightNet, where
I focus on performance engineering, system architecture, and developer experience. I also look after
our CI, testing, and documentation systems that keep these projects healthy and usable by non-technical
stakeholders, largely through bespoke tooling.

On the academic side, I've presented research on the recurence of ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS)
at the American Association for Cancer Research's (AACR) annual conference in Chicago (2025). I've
also done statistical analysis and modeling work for other research groups in the cancer research
field, and have been included in their work, although I'm winding down from clinical adjacencies.

Outside of work, I songwrite, and (used to) play guitar. I am learning the drums, and enjoy running,
hiking, and camping around the intermountain west. I'd love to get back into backcountry and
sidecountry skiing, if it weren't so expensive!

I am currently an honors student at the University of Utah, pursuing a dual undergraduate degree
in computer science and biology, with an emphasis on ecology, evolution, and environmental biology.

You can find me on [SourceHut](https://sr.ht/~oliviabanks) and [GitHub](https://github.com/olivia-banks).
I sometimes talk on [the orange site](https://news.ycombinator.com/threads?id=olivia-banks).
